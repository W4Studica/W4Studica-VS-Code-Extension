Inherited extension material
----------------------------
The repository began with Scott Libert's VMXVSCodeExtension_studica_wsr,
commit 5e8cf42 (2021-02-01). No project license or blanket redistribution
permission was found in the original tracked source, package manifest, or
README. Public source availability is not itself a license grant.

The original icon resources/VMX_WSR_128.png remains upstream material and is
excluded from new VSIX packages. Other inherited files include the original
extension/configuration scaffolding and history. W4Studica's MIT grant covers
only its own additions and changes; it does not authorize use or redistribution
of inherited material. Permission from its rightsholder is still unresolved.

The extension downloads GradleRIO and WPILib separately. Those distributions
retain their own licenses and are not covered by this extension's MIT grant.
VMX HAL headers and binaries are not included in the release bundles.
